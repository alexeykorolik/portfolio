Selected background
Space BSU × MTBank Hackathon

Career value beyond payday.

Jury's Choice Award · Speaker

Developed the core concept for a career platform integrated directly into MTBank's mobile banking experience.

The product was designed around young professionals using their first salary card. We proposed an AI-powered assistant that matched users with relevant job opportunities based on their preferences and career criteria, giving them an additional reason to remain within the bank's ecosystem as their careers developed.

My contribution included product concept development, UX/UI design in Figma, adapting the AI model for the vacancy-matching use case, and preparing the final product presentation. I presented the solution to the jury.

The project received the Jury's Choice Award.
